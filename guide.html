4. Telegram bot (built in)

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, pick a name, and copy the token it gives you.
  2. On dashboard.render.com, open your service, then Environment, and add:
    TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN = the token
    TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_IDS = your Telegram ID (leave empty for now)
  3. Save. Render restarts the service (about 2 minutes).
  4. Message your bot. It replies with your Telegram ID. Put that number in TELEGRAM_ALLOWED_IDS (several people: 111,222) and save again.
  5. Done. Send any message. Commands: /new, /think on, /search on, /status.

Only IDs in the list can use the bot. Anyone else just sees their own ID. This protects your account.

Good to know

  • Requests are answered one at a time, so many users at once will queue.
  • Think and Search replies take longer.
  • Every API call opens a new chat in your DeepSeek account history.
