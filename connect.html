Connect your DeepSeek account

Sign in with your own Chrome, where your phone's Google accounts are already listed. Then send that login to your site in one step.

Step 1. Make your one-time code.

Code is valid for 10 minutes. Tap Copy now.

Step 2. In Chrome, open a new tab and go to chat.deepseek.com. Choose Sign in with Google and pick your account.
Step 3. When you can see the DeepSeek chat box:
  1. Tap the address bar at the top of Chrome.
  2. Type javascript: (Chrome removes it if you paste it, so type it).
  3. Paste the copied text right after it, then press Go.
You'll land on a page that says Success. Then go to your chat and talk.
Bookmark way (set up once, use any time)

No code needed. On Android, tapping a bookmark in the list does nothing. You must type its name in the address bar and tap it there.

  1. Open this page, tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark it.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Bookmarks → long-press that bookmark → Edit.
  3. Change the name to ds.
  4. Clear the URL, type javascript: by hand, then paste the copied text. Save.
  5. Sign in at chat.deepseek.com. Tap the address bar, type ds, and tap the suggestion that has a bookmark icon.
